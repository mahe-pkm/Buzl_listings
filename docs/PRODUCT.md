# Product Specification — Phase 1 Direction

## Product

Buzl Business Listing is a citation-first public business directory.

## Primary initial audience

- Buzl Digital Solutions clients
- Buzl members

## Later audience

- External businesses registering directly

## Primary job

Create a stable, public, indexable business profile containing accurate business citation information.

## Confirmed product boundaries

- One listing represents one storefront, service-area, or hybrid business presence.
- A business can hide its exact street address while retaining structured internal address data and public service areas.
- The owner-facing NAP record is canonical; normalized values exist only for matching and validation.
- Categories are curated and hierarchical. Services describe offerings; tags are controlled cross-cutting descriptors.
- Buzl client and owner submissions are the source of truth. The platform does not build its inventory from scraped directory data.
- A listing can be public without being verified, and a verified listing can be suspended.

## MVP

- authentication
- business onboarding
- business management
- NAP/contact data
- categories
- tags
- services
- business hours
- location/map
- public listing page
- basic directory search
- SEO metadata
- LocalBusiness structured data
- sitemap/robots
- admin foundation

## Not MVP

- consumer reviews
- paid ads marketplace
- lead marketplace
- booking engine
- chat
- native mobile app
- complex recommendation/ranking AI

## Phase 2 specification work

Before implementation, lock the exact business fields, onboarding credential method, taxonomy seed, map provider, public-page quality thresholds, schema, RLS policies, and moderation rules.
